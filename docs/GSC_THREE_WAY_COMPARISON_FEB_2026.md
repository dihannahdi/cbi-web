## 📈 Which Data Should You Trust for Decisions?

### **Priority 1: Google Search Console (Primary Source)**

**Why GSC is MOST important:**
- ✅ **Real user clicks** - Not simulated/estimated
- ✅ **Real impressions** - Actual SERP appearances
- ✅ **Real CTR** - Shows how compelling your snippets are
- ✅ **Personalized for YOUR audience** - Represents qualified traffic

**Use GSC for:**
- Tracking performance improvements
- Identifying which pages/queries drive traffic
- Measuring CTR and optimizing meta descriptions
- Understanding user search intent
- **Making strategic SEO decisions**

**GSC Limitations:**
- Filters low-volume keywords (<20-50 impressions)
- Won't show every keyword you rank for
- 16-month data retention only

---

### **Priority 2: Ahrefs (Secondary Source)**

**Why Ahrefs is useful:**
- ✅ **Reveals hidden rankings** - Shows low-volume keywords GSC filters
- ✅ **Competitive research** - See competitor positions
- ✅ **Keyword discovery** - Find new opportunities
- ✅ **Consistent methodology** - Good for tracking trends

**Use Ahrefs for:**
- Finding keywords GSC doesn't show
- Analyzing competitor strategies
- Estimating search volumes
- Discovering long-tail opportunities
- Validating GSC data

**Ahrefs Limitations:**
- Not real user data (simulated crawls)
- May not reflect personalization
- Position estimates can vary ±3-5 positions

---

### **Priority 3: Manual Checks (Validation Only)**

**Why manual checks have LIMITED value:**
- ⚠️ **Depersonalized** - Doesn't reflect how users see results
- ⚠️ **Location-specific** - May not represent target audience location
- ⚠️ **Time-sensitive** - SERPs change hourly
- ⚠️ **Device-specific** - Mobile vs desktop differ
- ⚠️ **Ads affect positions** - Organic position ≠ visual position

**Use manual checks for:**
- Quick spot-checks of specific keywords
- Validating SERP features (featured snippets, knowledge panels)
- Checking competitor presence
- **NOT** for strategic decisions

**DON'T:**
- ❌ Panic over low manual positions when GSC shows success
- ❌ Trust manual checks over GSC data
- ❌ Use manual positions for performance tracking
